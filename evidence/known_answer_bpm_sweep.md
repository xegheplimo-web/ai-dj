**Reading the table:**
1. `IBI (frames)` is an **exact integer** in every single row — the snap is directly visible.
2. Within one ladder bucket the analyzer reports the **bucket's** BPM no matter what the truth is
   (truth 120 -> 117.454, 126 -> 123.047, 132 -> 129.199, 140 -> 143.555, 148 -> 151.999).
3. **Octave errors** (ratio ~0.5): truth 166.0 / 166.686 / 168.0 all reported as **83.354**;
   truth 178.0 / 180.0 both reported as **89.103**. These are ~50% wrong.
4. `tempo_confidence` is **0.9 on 2.5% errors** and 0.63 on 50% errors — it never separates
   right from wrong. It measures *regularity* (ibi_cv), not *correctness*.
5. The control (truth exactly on the ladder, 161.484) is accurate to **0.009%** — the analyzer
   is only trustworthy when the true tempo happens to land on a ladder value.
